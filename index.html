About Me

Combining modern frontend craftsmanship with resilient backend architecture

I'm a software engineering apprentice who thrives on solving problems with code. My journey started at Kiriri Women's University, where I discovered not just a passion for technology, but a calling — to be a woman in tech in a space that's still too often seen as a man's world.

After university, I took on Piscine — a grueling month-long coding bootcamp that pushed me to my limits. It was there I learned that I belonged. Now at Zone01 Kisumu, I'm building real-world applications with Go, JavaScript, Docker, and PostgreSQL.

As a woman in tech, I bring a perspective that the industry needs more of — resilience, empathy, and the drive to build things that matter. I'm looking for opportunities where I can contribute, grow, and prove that talent has no gender.
